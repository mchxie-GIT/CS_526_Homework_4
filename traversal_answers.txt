CS526 Homework Assignment 4

Problem 1: General tree

Preorder:      A B E H F I C D G
Breadth-first: A B C D E F G H I
Postorder:     H E I F B C G D A

Preorder visits the node before its children. Breadth-first visits nodes
level by level. Postorder visits the children before the node.
Children are visited from left to right.

Problem 1: Binary tree (second Problem 1 in the assignment)

In-order: D G B E A C H F I

In-order visits the left subtree, then the node, then the right subtree.
